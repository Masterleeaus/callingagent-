# Cleaning Service Rate Card

This rate card defines all standard pricing for cleaning services. Prices shown are in AUD (inclusive of GST) and are effective from 1 November 2024. All quotes generated by the BookingAgent use these rates.

---

## Residential Cleaning — Hourly Rates

Hourly rates apply when the job is time-based (regular maintenance cleans, custom requests).

| Region Band  | Standard Rate | After-Hours / Weekend | Public Holiday |
|--------------|---------------|-----------------------|----------------|
| Metro A      | $55/hr        | $72/hr                | $95/hr         |
| Metro B      | $50/hr        | $65/hr                | $88/hr         |
| Outer Metro  | $47/hr        | $60/hr                | $82/hr         |
| Regional     | $44/hr + travel | $58/hr + travel     | $78/hr + travel |

**After-hours** = any booking starting after 6:00 PM or before 7:00 AM weekdays.
**Weekend** = Saturday and Sunday, all day.
**Public Holiday** = state/territory public holidays.

## Residential Cleaning — Fixed Prices

Fixed prices provide certainty for customers. They are calculated from the estimating guide formulas and locked at booking time. Fixed-price bookings include all labour; extras are billed separately.

### Regular / Maintenance Clean (Fixed Price)

| Property Size            | Price Range  |
|--------------------------|--------------|
| Studio / 1 bed, 1 bath   | $110 – $140  |
| 2 bed, 1 bath            | $140 – $175  |
| 2 bed, 2 bath            | $165 – $200  |
| 3 bed, 1 bath            | $175 – $215  |
| 3 bed, 2 bath            | $200 – $250  |
| 4 bed, 2 bath            | $260 – $310  |
| 4 bed, 3 bath            | $295 – $355  |
| 5 bed, 3 bath            | $350 – $420  |

*Prices assume **standard condition**. Apply condition modifiers for light (−20%) or heavy (+30%) properties.*

### Recurring Discount
- **Weekly:** 15% off the fixed price.
- **Fortnightly:** 10% off the fixed price.
- **Monthly:** 5% off the fixed price.

## End-of-Lease (Bond / Vacate) Clean Rates

End-of-lease cleans are fixed price and include all rooms, surfaces, and fixtures to real estate inspection standard. Kitchen appliances (oven, range hood, stovetop) are included in all end-of-lease cleans.

| Property Size            | Price (incl. kitchen appliances) |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Studio / 1 bed, 1 bath   | $220 – $280                      |
| 2 bed, 1 bath            | $300 – $370                      |
| 2 bed, 2 bath            | $360 – $430                      |
| 3 bed, 1 bath            | $380 – $450                      |
| 3 bed, 2 bath            | $450 – $540                      |
| 4 bed, 2 bath            | $560 – $680                      |
| 4 bed, 3 bath            | $640 – $760                      |
| 5 bed, 3 bath            | $750 – $900                      |

**End-of-lease clean includes:** All rooms, all floors, all windows (interior), all cupboards (inside and out), all appliances in kitchen, all bathroom fixtures, light fittings, skirting boards, doors and door frames.

**Bond guarantee:** If the real estate agent fails the property on a cleaning item, we return for a free re-clean within 72 hours (conditions apply — see Service Policy).

| Period                        | Adjustment            |
|-------------------------------|-----------------------|

| Evening (5pm – 8pm)           | +15%                  |

---

## Price Validity

All quoted prices are valid for **7 days** from the date the quote is issued. After 7 days, a new quote must be generated. Seasonal adjustments (e.g., peak season surcharge) are applied at the time of booking confirmation, not at quote time.
