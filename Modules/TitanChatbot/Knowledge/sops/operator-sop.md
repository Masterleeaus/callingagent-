# Operator Standard Operating Procedure
## Cleaning Service Delivery

This SOP defines the standard procedure for all cleaning operators delivering services booked through TitanChatbot. All cleaners must be familiar with and follow this procedure for every job.

---

## 1. Pre-Clean Checklist

Complete the following before leaving for the job:

### Equipment Check
- [ ] Vacuum cleaner — charged, bag/filter clean
- [ ] Mop and bucket — clean
- [ ] Microfibre cloths (minimum 12 — colour-coded by zone)
- [ ] Scrubbing brushes and scouring pads
- [ ] Spray bottles — labelled and correctly filled
- [ ] Extension cord (if required for vacuum)
- [ ] Personal protective equipment (gloves, apron, non-slip footwear)

### Product Check
- [ ] All-purpose cleaner
- [ ] Bathroom disinfectant
- [ ] Glass/window cleaner
- [ ] Kitchen degreaser
- [ ] Toilet cleaner (with bleach)
- [ ] Floor cleaner (suitable for property's flooring type)
- [ ] Stainless steel polish (if kitchen has SS appliances)
- [ ] Odour neutraliser
- [ ] Specialty products as noted in job card (e.g., eco products, fragrance-free)

### Job Card Review
- [ ] Confirm property address and any access instructions
- [ ] Note service type (regular / end-of-lease / deep clean / etc.)
- [ ] Note any extras (oven, fridge, windows, carpet)
- [ ] Note special instructions or customer preferences
- [ ] Confirm scheduled start time and estimated duration
- [ ] Check for known pet presence — prepare accordingly

## 2. Arrival and Access Procedure

### On Arrival
1. Arrive within **5 minutes** of the scheduled start time. If delayed by more than 10 minutes, call the customer immediately.
2. If using a lock box or key: retrieve the key, confirm the address plate matches the job card.
3. Photograph the exterior of the property (front door/entry) with the timestamp on arrival.
4. Ring the doorbell or knock to confirm no one is home. Wait 30 seconds.

### Access Challenges
- **Customer not home, no key:** Call the office within **5 minutes**. Do not wait more than **15 minutes** without office guidance.
- **Lock box code fails:** Call the office immediately. Do not force entry under any circumstances.
- **Property inaccessible:** Take timestamped photos, call the office. Job is logged as a no-show; customer is billed per policy.
- **Pet unsecured:** Call the customer. If the pet cannot be secured within 5 minutes, clean accessible areas only and note which areas were inaccessible.

### Pre-Clean Property Assessment
Before starting, walk through the property and:
1. Photograph **any pre-existing damage** (scratches, stains, broken items, mould, damage to surfaces).
2. Upload photos to the job card in the app immediately.
3. If the property condition is significantly worse than the booking description (e.g., booked as "standard" but property is heavily soiled or contains biohazards), call the office before starting. Do not proceed without office authorisation.

## 3. Product Usage and Dilution Guidelines

### General Rules
- **Never mix bleach with ammonia-based cleaners** (produces toxic chloramine gas).
- **Never mix bleach with acidic cleaners** (e.g., some bathroom sprays).
- Always read product labels before first use.
- Dilute concentrates as per the manufacturer's instructions. Never use full-strength unless specified.

### Standard Dilution Ratios

| Product                | Use Case                  | Dilution Ratio        |
|------------------------|---------------------------|-----------------------|
| All-purpose cleaner    | General surfaces          | 1:10 (product:water)  |
| Kitchen degreaser      | Heavy grease              | 1:5 (heavy), 1:15 (light) |
| Bathroom disinfectant  | Toilets, sinks, tiles     | As per label (usually ready-to-use) |
| Floor cleaner          | Hard floors               | 1:20                  |
| Glass cleaner          | Windows, mirrors          | Ready-to-use          |

### Surface-Specific Guidelines
- **Granite / marble:** Never use acidic cleaners. Use neutral pH products only.
- **Timber floors:** Damp mop only — do not soak. Use appropriate timber floor cleaner.
- **Stainless steel:** Always wipe in the direction of the grain. Polish last.
- **Glass shower screens:** Use dedicated glass cleaner + squeegee. Never use abrasive pads.
- **Tile grout:** Use grout brush with diluted bleach solution. Rinse thoroughly.

## 4. Cleaning Order — Recommended Sequence

To avoid cross-contamination and re-soiling, always clean in this order:

1. **Dust from top to bottom** — ceilings, light fittings, ceiling fans, cornices, wall art, shelves.
2. **Spray and soak** — apply bathroom and kitchen cleaners to surfaces that need dwell time; let sit while cleaning elsewhere.
3. **Bedrooms** — dust, wipe surfaces, vacuum/mop.
4. **Living areas** — dust, wipe, vacuum/mop.
5. **Kitchen** — degrease and wipe all surfaces, appliances, sink; mop last.
6. **Bathrooms** — scrub and disinfect, toilets last; mop floors last.
7. **Entry / hallways / stairs** — vacuum/mop last as you work toward the exit.
8. **Extras** (oven, fridge, windows, carpet) — complete before or after main clean as agreed.

## 5. Quality Inspection Steps

Before leaving the property, conduct a quality self-inspection:

### General
- [ ] No visible dust on any surfaces at eye level.
- [ ] No streaks on mirrors, glass surfaces, or stainless steel.
- [ ] No footprints on mopped floors.
- [ ] All bins emptied (if this is a service inclusion).
- [ ] All furniture and items returned to original position.
- [ ] No cleaning products or equipment left in the property.

### Room-Specific Spot Checks
- **Kitchen:** Stovetop shines, sink drain is clear, microwave clean inside/out.
- **Bathrooms:** Toilet flushed and clean, no soap scum on tiles, taps gleam.
- **Bedrooms:** Skirting boards wiped, wardrobe doors closed, bed made (if linen provided).
- **Living areas:** All cushions straightened, surfaces dust-free.

---

## 6. Post-Clean Sign-Off

1. Take **completion photos** of key areas (kitchen, bathroom, main living area) with timestamp.
2. Lock and secure the property using the same method as entry.
3. Return keys to the secure location or lock box as instructed.
4. Mark the job as **completed** in the app within **5 minutes** of leaving.
5. Submit photos and any notes in the job card.
6. If a re-clean or quality issue was identified, flag in the job notes immediately.

---

## 7. Incident Reporting

### What Must Be Reported
- Any **damage** to the property (accidental breakage, spill damage, surface scratching).
- Any **safety incident** (cleaner injury, hazardous material found, slip/fall).
- Any **security concern** (property left unsecured, suspicious persons, unusual access requests).
- Any **customer complaint** raised in person during or after the service.

### How to Report
1. Photograph the incident immediately with timestamp.
2. Do not admit fault or liability to the customer.
3. Call the office within **15 minutes** of the incident.
4. Complete the Incident Report form in the app before the end of the work day.

### Damage Protocol
If you accidentally damage something:
- Take photos.
- Call the office immediately (not the customer).
- The office will contact the customer.
- Do not offer personal compensation or make promises about outcomes.
