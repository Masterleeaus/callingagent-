# Sample Residential Cleaning Service Agreement

> **Notice:** This document is a sample outline for informational purposes only. It is not a legally binding document and does not constitute legal advice. Operators should have their service agreement reviewed by a qualified solicitor before use. The BookingAgent must not quote specific clauses from this document to customers; it must direct customers to review the full agreement or escalate to a human.

---

## Parties

This Agreement is between:

- **Service Provider:** [Business Name], ABN [ABN], of [Business Address] ("we", "us", "the Company").
- **Customer:** The individual or entity named in the Booking Confirmation ("you", "the Customer").

---

## 1. Scope of Services

### 1.1 Services Covered
We agree to provide the cleaning services specified in the Booking Confirmation, including service type, property address, scheduled date and time, and any agreed add-ons.

### 1.2 Changes to Scope
Any changes to the scope of services (additional rooms, extra services, change of service type) must be agreed in writing before the service commences. Verbal changes on the day cannot be guaranteed and may not be completed if resources are insufficient.

### 1.3 Exclusions
Unless expressly included in the Booking Confirmation, the following are excluded from all services:
- Cleaning at heights above 2 metres.
- Removal of heavy furniture or appliances.
- Cleaning of biohazardous material, asbestos, or mould that poses a health risk.
- Pest control or extermination.
- Any work requiring a licensed trade (plumbing, electrical).

---

## 2. Payment Terms

### 2.1 Price
The price for the services is as stated in the Booking Confirmation. All prices are in Australian Dollars (AUD) and include GST.

### 2.2 Payment Method
Payment is collected by credit card at the time of booking confirmation, or by invoice with 7-day terms for approved commercial accounts.

### 2.3 Additional Charges
If additional work is identified on-site and agreed by the Customer, a supplementary invoice will be issued. If the Customer does not agree, the additional work will not be performed.

### 2.4 Late Payment
Invoices unpaid after 14 days incur a 1.5% monthly late fee. We reserve the right to suspend recurring services for accounts with outstanding invoices.

---

## 3. Cancellation and Rescheduling

### 3.1 Customer Cancellation
See Service Policy (cancellation windows and fees apply as stated therein).

### 3.2 Company Cancellation
If we are unable to fulfil a booking due to unforeseen circumstances (illness, equipment failure, natural disaster), we will notify the Customer as soon as practicable and offer a full refund or priority rescheduling.

### 3.3 Right to Refuse Service
We reserve the right to refuse or cease service if: (a) the property poses a health or safety risk to our cleaners; (b) the Customer or any person at the property is aggressive or threatening; (c) the property condition is substantially worse than described at booking and no revised agreement is reached.

---

## 4. Liability

### 4.1 Our Liability
We will exercise reasonable care and skill in delivering the services. If damage occurs to your property due to our negligence, we will assess the claim and repair or compensate up to the limits of our public liability insurance.

### 4.2 Limitation of Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by law, our total liability for any claim arising from services is limited to the amount paid for the affected service.

### 4.3 Customer's Liability
The Customer is responsible for:
- Securing or removing pets before the service.
- Advising us of any fragile, valuable, or unusual items that require special care.
- Ensuring the property is safe for our cleaners (no hazardous materials, working utilities).
- Any damage caused by access codes or keys provided incorrectly.

### 4.4 Pre-Existing Damage
We are not liable for damage to items that were already damaged or in a fragile state prior to the service. If a cleaner identifies pre-existing damage, they will photograph it and note it in the job record.

---

## 5. Insurance

We hold the following insurance policies:

- **Public Liability Insurance:** $20,000,000 AUD.
- **Workers' Compensation:** As required by applicable state law.
- **Products Liability:** Included in the public liability policy.

Certificates of currency are available on request.

---

## 6. Privacy and Data

We collect and use your personal information in accordance with our Privacy Policy and the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Your information is used solely for delivering and communicating about services and is not sold to third parties.

---

## 7. Dispute Resolution

In the event of a dispute, the parties agree to first attempt resolution through the process outlined in the Service Policy (Steps 1–5). If unresolved, disputes will be referred to the relevant state Fair Trading / Consumer Affairs body for mediation before any legal action is pursued.

---

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state or territory in which the service is delivered. Both parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of that jurisdiction.

---

## 9. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the Booking Confirmation and Service Policy, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior representations, understandings, or agreements.
