# Room-by-Room Quality Inspection Checklist

This checklist is used by cleaners and quality inspectors to verify a clean is complete and to the required standard. Mark each item as ✅ (pass), ⚠️ (needs attention), or ❌ (failed / not done).

---

## Kitchen

### Surfaces
- [ ] Benchtops — wiped, streak-free, no food residue
- [ ] Splashback — degreased, grout lines clean
- [ ] Rangehood — exterior wiped, filters clean (end-of-lease/deep clean: filters removed and cleaned)
- [ ] Cabinet doors (exterior) — wiped, no greasy fingerprints
- [ ] Cabinet interiors (end-of-lease/deep clean: shelves wiped, no crumbs or residue)
- [ ] Drawers (end-of-lease: interiors wiped)
- [ ] Window sill (kitchen) — dusted and wiped

### Appliances
- [ ] Stovetop — all burners or elements clean, no baked-on residue
- [ ] Oven (if included) — interior cavity, oven racks, door glass and seals clean
- [ ] Microwave — interior (ceiling, walls, turntable), exterior
- [ ] Fridge exterior — wiped, no smudges
- [ ] Fridge interior (if included) — shelves, drawers, door seals
- [ ] Dishwasher exterior — wiped
- [ ] Kettle and toaster (exterior only) — wiped

### Sink and Fixtures
- [ ] Sink — scrubbed, stain-free, drain clear
- [ ] Taps — polished, no water spots or lime scale
- [ ] Waste bin — emptied and liner replaced (if service includes)

### Floors
- [ ] Swept or vacuumed — no crumbs, debris
- [ ] Mopped — streak-free, no dirty water marks
- [ ] Under kickboards and behind doors — swept

---

## Bathrooms (repeat for each bathroom)

**Bathroom #: ______**

### Toilet
- [ ] Exterior — lid, tank, base, and behind toilet
- [ ] Seat and lid — both sides
- [ ] Interior bowl — under rim, below water line, no staining or lime scale
- [ ] Floor around base — cleaned

### Sink
- [ ] Basin — scrubbed, no soap scum or staining
- [ ] Taps — polished, no water spots
- [ ] Drain — clear
- [ ] Vanity surface — wiped
- [ ] Vanity mirror — streak-free, no toothpaste marks
- [ ] Vanity cabinet (end-of-lease: interior wiped)

### Shower / Bath
- [ ] Shower screen — no soap scum, water spots, or streaks
- [ ] Tiles — scrubbed, grout lines clean, no mould visible
- [ ] Shower head — wiped, no mineral build-up
- [ ] Shower tray / base — scrubbed, no hair or residue
- [ ] Bath (if present) — interior and exterior scrubbed, taps polished
- [ ] Overflow drain — cleared

### General Bathroom
- [ ] Exhaust fan cover — dust-free
- [ ] Towel rails — wiped
- [ ] Toilet paper holder — wiped
- [ ] Bin — emptied, liner replaced
- [ ] Skirting boards — wiped
- [ ] Floor — swept/vacuumed, mopped (no dirty water marks)

---

## Bedrooms (repeat for each bedroom)

**Bedroom #: ______**

### Surfaces and Dusting
- [ ] Ceiling fan or light fitting — dust-free
- [ ] Window sills — dusted and wiped
- [ ] Skirting boards — dust-free
- [ ] Bedside tables — wiped, lamp base dusted
- [ ] Dresser / chest of drawers — exterior wiped
- [ ] Wardrobe exterior (doors and top) — wiped
- [ ] Wardrobe interior (end-of-lease: shelves, rails, base wiped, no dust or debris)
- [ ] Any shelving — dust-free
- [ ] Mirrors — streak-free

### Bed and Soft Furnishings
- [ ] Bed made (if customer has provided fresh linen)
- [ ] Pillows straightened
- [ ] Under bed — vacuumed or swept (accessible area)

### Floors
- [ ] Vacuumed — all areas, including under and around furniture where accessible
- [ ] Hard floors: mopped (if applicable)
- [ ] No dust bunnies in corners

---

## Living Areas

### Surfaces
- [ ] Ceiling fan / light fittings — dust-free
- [ ] Window sills — wiped
- [ ] Skirting boards — wiped or dusted
- [ ] Entertainment unit / TV cabinet — dusted (top and sides; not behind TV unless requested)
- [ ] Coffee table — wiped
- [ ] Dining table and chairs — wiped
- [ ] Shelves — all accessible surfaces dusted
- [ ] Any ornaments or picture frames — dusted (carefully)

### Soft Furnishings
- [ ] Sofa / couch — vacuumed (surface cushions and edges)
- [ ] Cushions — straightened
- [ ] Blinds or curtains — dusted or wiped (if included)

### Floors
- [ ] Vacuumed — all areas including corners
- [ ] Hard floors: mopped
- [ ] Rugs — vacuumed (both sides if removable, standard service = top surface)

---

## Entry / Hallways

- [ ] Front door (interior) — wiped, door frame dusted
- [ ] Entry floor mat — shaken out (outside) and replaced
- [ ] Skirting boards — wiped
- [ ] Any hall table or furniture — dusted and wiped
- [ ] Staircase (if present):
  - [ ] Steps — vacuumed or swept
  - [ ] Balustrade / handrail — wiped
  - [ ] Skirting along stairs — dusted
- [ ] Light switches and power points — wiped
- [ ] Floor — vacuumed / mopped last (work toward the exit)

---

## End-of-Lease Final Check

The following additional items are checked for end-of-lease cleans only:

### Throughout the Property
- [ ] All light switches and power points — clean, no greasy marks
- [ ] All door handles — polished
- [ ] All doors (both sides, top edge) — wiped
- [ ] All door frames and architraves — wiped
- [ ] All skirting boards (full length) — wiped, no dust build-up
- [ ] All interior windows (glass, sill, tracks, frame) — clean and streak-free
- [ ] All blinds (horizontal: wiped; vertical: wiped or specialist-cleaned if booked)
- [ ] All ceiling light fittings — cleaned (remove cover if possible to clean insects out)
- [ ] All wall vents / air-conditioning filters — dusted (filters: removable and washable only)
- [ ] No marks, grime, or sticky residue on any walls (spot-clean included)
- [ ] All rubbish removed from property
- [ ] All keys returned to designated location

### Real Estate Readiness Sign-Off

Cleaner confirms:
- Property is ready for a real estate inspection.
- All agreed items have been completed.
- Any incomplete items (e.g., items requiring specialist treatment) have been documented and the office notified.
