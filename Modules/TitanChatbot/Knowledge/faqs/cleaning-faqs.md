# Cleaning Service FAQs

Answers to the most common questions from customers. The BookingAgent uses this as its primary source for answering general enquiries.

---

## What's Included in a Standard Clean

### Q1: What is included in a standard clean?
A standard (regular maintenance) clean includes:
- **Kitchen:** Benchtops wiped, stovetop cleaned (exterior), sink and taps cleaned, cabinet exteriors wiped, microwave (exterior), floors swept and mopped.
- **Bathrooms:** Toilet (inside and out), sink and taps, shower screen and tiles, bath (if present), mirrors, floors.
- **Bedrooms:** Dusting of surfaces, window sills, skirting boards, vacuuming floors, making beds (with fresh linen if provided).
- **Living areas:** Dusting all surfaces, vacuuming upholstered furniture (surface only), vacuuming/mopping floors.
- **Entry / hallways:** Vacuuming or mopping, dusting light switches and door frames.

**Not included** in standard clean: Inside ovens, inside fridges, interior windows, carpet steam cleaning, walls, garage, balcony (unless added as extras).

### Q2: What is NOT included in a standard clean?
Inside ovens, inside fridges, inside dishwashers, interior cupboards (standard service cleans the outside only), window interiors, blinds, walls, carpet deep cleaning, garage, balcony, outdoor areas. These can be added as extras — see the rate card for pricing.

### Q3: What does an end-of-lease clean include?
An end-of-lease clean is our most comprehensive service and includes everything in the standard clean PLUS: inside oven and range hood, inside all kitchen cupboards and drawers, all interior windows, inside fridge (if still in property), inside wardrobes, all light fittings and switches, skirting boards (full clean), door frames and doors (both sides), all floors (vacuumed and mopped). We aim to meet real estate agent inspection standards.

### Q4: What does a deep clean include?
A deep clean covers all areas of the standard clean, with extra attention to grime, grease, and build-up. It includes inside the oven, inside the range hood filter, detailed tile and grout scrubbing in bathrooms, full skirting board clean, and thorough dusting of all surfaces including blinds and ceiling fans.

## Booking and Rescheduling

### Q5: How do I make a booking?
You can book through this chat, on our website, by calling our office, or via WhatsApp. The agent will take your property details, preferred date, and confirm a price before confirming your booking.

### Q6: How far in advance do I need to book?
For regular cleans, we recommend booking at least **3 days in advance**. For end-of-lease cleans, we recommend **5–7 days** as slots fill quickly. Same-day bookings may be available — ask the agent to check availability.

### Q7: Can I reschedule my booking?
Yes. Reschedules are free with more than 48 hours' notice. With 24–48 hours' notice, one free reschedule is allowed. Less than 24 hours' notice is treated as a late cancellation and fees may apply. See our Service Policy for full details.

### Q8: Can I cancel my booking?
Yes. Cancellations with more than 48 hours' notice receive a full refund. Cancellations between 24 and 48 hours incur a 50% fee. Less than 24 hours' notice results in the full booking fee being charged. First-time customers receive a one-off waiver for late cancellations.

### Q9: What happens if I miss my appointment?
If our cleaner arrives and cannot access the property, they will wait up to 15 minutes and attempt to contact you. If they cannot gain access, the booking is treated as a no-show and the full fee plus a $35 lock-out fee is charged.

### Q10: Can I get a recurring booking?
Absolutely. We offer weekly, fortnightly, and monthly recurring bookings with discounts of 15%, 10%, and 5% respectively. You can set up a recurring booking through the agent and cancel or pause at any time with the standard notice period.

### Q11: Can I book for the same day?
Same-day bookings are subject to availability. Ask the agent to check — we do our best to accommodate last-minute requests, especially for regular maintenance cleans.

## Products and Equipment

### Q12: Do you supply your own cleaning products and equipment?
Yes. Our cleaners bring all necessary professional-grade cleaning products and equipment, including mops, vacuum cleaners, microfibre cloths, and all cleaning solutions. You do not need to provide anything.

### Q13: What cleaning products do you use?
We use professional, biodegradable cleaning products that are effective on tough grime but safe for surfaces, children, and pets. We use hospital-grade disinfectants in bathrooms and food-safe products in kitchens.

### Q14: Can I request eco-friendly or fragrance-free products?
Yes. We offer an eco-friendly product range (plant-based, no synthetic fragrances) and fragrance-free options for customers with sensitivities. Please note this when booking — there is no extra charge.

### Q15: Can I provide my own products?
Yes, but we recommend using ours. If you have specific products you prefer (e.g., for a particular surface type), please leave them accessible with clear instructions. Our cleaners are trained on our standard products and may decline to use unfamiliar chemicals.

### Q16: Do your products contain bleach?
Our standard products do not use undiluted bleach in living areas. Diluted bleach solution may be used in bathrooms and toilets for disinfection and mould treatment. If you have concerns, please advise us when booking.

## Key and Access Requirements

### Q17: Do I need to be home when the cleaner comes?
No, you don't need to be home. Most of our customers prefer a key handover, a lock box code, or access via the building manager. Just make sure all areas to be cleaned are accessible.

### Q18: How do I give the cleaner access if I'm not home?
Options include: (1) Leave a key under the doormat or in a nominated secure spot and advise us. (2) Use a key lock box — provide the code when booking. (3) Arrange access via a building manager or real estate agent. (4) Use a smart lock and provide a one-time code.

### Q19: Is my key safe?
Yes. Keys are never taken off-site and are never copied. If a lock box is used, codes are encrypted in our booking system. For high-security properties, we recommend a one-time smart lock code.

### Q20: Do I need to provide parking?
Cleaners will need access to a parking spot within a reasonable distance of the property. If parking is metered or requires a permit, please arrange this and let us know. Parking costs incurred by the cleaner are passed on to the customer.

## Payment and Invoicing

### Q21: How do I pay?
We accept credit/debit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), bank transfer, and digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay). Payment is taken at the time of booking or via invoice for commercial clients.

### Q22: When do I get charged?
For individual bookings, the card is charged at the time of booking confirmation. For recurring bookings, charges are made 24 hours before each scheduled service.

### Q23: Can I get an invoice?
Yes. An invoice/receipt is automatically emailed after each service. For business customers requiring tax invoices, please provide your ABN/business name at the time of booking.

### Q24: What if I'm not happy with the service — do I still have to pay?
If you report an issue within 48 hours, we will arrange a free re-clean of the disputed areas. You do not need to dispute the charge if you raise the issue promptly — we resolve it at our cost. If you believe a refund is warranted, please contact our office or ask the agent to escalate.

### Q25: Do you charge extra for EFTPOS / card payments?
No. We do not pass on card surcharges. The price you are quoted is the price you pay.

## Quality Guarantee

### Q26: What is your quality guarantee?
We guarantee the quality of every clean. If you're not satisfied with any aspect of the service, contact us within 48 hours and we'll return to re-clean the affected areas at no charge.

### Q27: What does the guarantee cover?
The guarantee covers all work performed by our cleaners during the booked service. It applies to specific areas reported as unsatisfactory, not necessarily the entire property.

### Q28: What is NOT covered by the guarantee?
The guarantee does not apply if: (1) the property has been re-occupied or used after the clean (e.g., cooking in a cleaned kitchen); (2) the issue is due to pre-existing damage, permanent staining, or surface deterioration that cannot be cleaned; (3) the claim is made more than 48 hours after the service.

## Pets and Allergens

### Q33: Do I need to secure my pets?
Yes. For the safety of both your pets and our cleaners, all pets must be secured or removed from the premises during the service. Pets that cannot be secured may result in the cleaner being unable to complete areas of the property.

### Q34: Will cleaning products affect my pets?
Our products are pet-safe once dry (typically 15–30 minutes after application). We recommend keeping pets out of cleaned areas until surfaces have fully dried. If your pet has specific sensitivities, please advise us and we can use fragrance-free, pet-certified products.

### Q35: Do your cleaners have allergies?
Some cleaners may have allergies to pets. If you have a pet in the property, please advise us at booking so we can assign an appropriate cleaner.

### Q36: Can you clean areas affected by pet odour?
Our standard products address surface odours. For deep pet odour treatment (subfloor, carpet, upholstery), we recommend a specialist odour treatment service — ask the agent if this is available in your area.
